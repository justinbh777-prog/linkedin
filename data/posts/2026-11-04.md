# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The valuation mistake I see franchise owners make over and over

---

Most franchise owners think their business is worth more than it is.

And it's not their fault.

They've spent years building something. They know the hours they put in. So when they imagine selling, they attach a number to all that effort.

But buyers don't pay for effort. They pay for profit.

Here's the mistake I see over and over. Owners value their business off revenue. "I did $1.2 million last year." Great. But what fell to the bottom line?

A buyer cares about one thing: what the business earns after you pay yourself a fair wage and cover real expenses.

I had an owner tell me his three units were worth $900K. When we actually looked at the books, his add-backs were sloppy and his manager costs were buried. The real number was lower, but we found ways to clean it up and get closer.

The lesson is simple. Clean financials are worth more than a big top-line number.

If you know your profit, you know your value.
