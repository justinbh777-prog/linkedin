# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why building a business that runs without you is the best exit strategy

---

The best time to build your exit strategy is years before you sell.

Here is what most owners miss.

Buyers are not paying for your business. They are paying for what your business does without you.

Over 20 years, I have watched two nearly identical franchises sell for very different numbers. Same revenue. Same market. Same brand.

The difference was one owner.

One of them ran everything. He opened the store, handled payroll, knew every customer by name. Take him out and the business wobbled.

The other had a manager, clear systems, and a team that ran the day to day. He could disappear for a month and nothing changed.

Guess which one got the higher offer. It was not close.

When you are the business, buyers see risk. When the business runs on its own, buyers see an asset.

So if you are years away from selling, start now.

Hire the manager. Write down the systems. Make yourself less important, on purpose.

Your future sale price will thank you.
