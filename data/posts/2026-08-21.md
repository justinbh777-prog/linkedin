# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The conversation every franchise owner should have 3 years before selling

---

Most owners start planning their exit two years too late.

I see it all the time. Someone calls me ready to sell, and the business is running great. Problem is, "running great" and "ready to sell" are not the same thing.

Here's the conversation nobody has early enough.

Three years before you sell, sit down and ask one question: is this business built around me, or around a system?

Buyers pay more for a business that runs without the owner. They pay less for a job you're trying to hand off.

I worked with an owner who did $2M in revenue but signed every check and knew every customer by name. Great operator. Hard sell. We spent two years building a management layer before we went to market. That work added real money to his final number.

The fix takes time. That's the whole point.

If you wait until you're tired and ready to be done, you've already left money on the table.

Start the conversation while you still have runway to act on it.
