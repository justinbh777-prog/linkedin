# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The valuation mistake I see franchise owners make over and over

---

Most franchise owners think their business is worth more than it is.

Not because they're wrong about the hard work. Because they're looking at the wrong number.

Here's the mistake I see again and again.

Owners value their business off revenue. "We did $2 million in sales last year." That feels big. But buyers don't buy revenue. They buy profit.

What actually matters is your adjusted earnings. What's left after real expenses, plus the owner perks we add back in.

I had an owner recently who was sure his three units were worth top dollar. Strong sales. Busy locations. But once we looked at the actual profit, the number was less than half of what he expected.

The units were busy. They just weren't making much.

The lesson is simple. Sales tell a story. Profit tells the truth. And profit is what sets your price.

If you don't know your adjusted earnings, you don't know your value. That's step one, every time.
