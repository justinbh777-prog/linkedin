# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why franchise owners underestimate how long a sale takes

---

"Just sell it by summer."

That's what an owner told me in January. He wanted to be out before his busy season ended.

He closed 14 months later.

Here's what most franchise owners don't understand. A sale isn't one event. It's a stack of steps, and each one takes longer than you think.

Finding the right buyer takes time. But that's just the start.

Then comes financing. Then the franchisor approval. Then lease transfers. Then due diligence, where buyers ask for numbers you haven't looked at in years.

Any one of these can add months.

The owner I mentioned wasn't slow or unprepared. He just assumed selling a business worked like selling a house. It doesn't.

The lesson isn't "start earlier because it's hard." It's this. The owners who get the best price are the ones who plan the exit years before they need it.

They fix the books. They clean up operations. They aren't rushing.

Rushing costs money. Every time.
