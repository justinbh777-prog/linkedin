# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What 100+ franchise transactions taught me about what buyers really want

---

Buyers don't buy your business. They buy your absence.

After 100+ franchise deals, that's the thing most owners miss.

They think a buyer wants high revenue. Strong brand. A good location.

Those help. But they're not what closes the deal.

What a buyer really wants is to know the business runs without you.

If you're the one answering every call, making every schedule, and fixing every problem, you're not selling a business. You're selling a job.

I had an owner once with great numbers. Clean books. Growing sales.

But he worked 60 hours a week and did everything himself. Buyers kept walking.

Then he spent a year building a manager and clear systems. Same business. Different story. It sold fast and for more.

Here's the takeaway.

The best thing you can do before you sell is make yourself replaceable.

Train your team. Write down your processes. Step back and watch what breaks.

Fix those things now, and you'll have options later.

The buyer isn't paying for what you built. They're paying to not need you.
