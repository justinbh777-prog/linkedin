**Topic:** What 100+ franchise transactions taught me about what buyers really want

---

Buyers don't buy your business. They buy their life after they own it.

That took me a while to figure out.

After 100+ franchise deals, I can tell you the number one thing buyers want isn't a low price. It's confidence that the business runs without you.

They're asking a quiet question the whole time: "Can I actually do this?"

The owners who sell fast, and for more, are the ones who built a business that doesn't live in their head.

Clean books. A team that stays. Systems written down somewhere other than the owner's memory.

The difference? The first owner could hand over a binder. The second one was the binder.

Here's the takeaway. Start acting like a buyer years before you sell. Ask yourself what someone would need to run this without you.
