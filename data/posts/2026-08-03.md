# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The conversation every franchise owner should have 3 years before selling

---

Most owners start planning their exit way too late.

By the time they call me, they want to sell in six months. That is when I have to give them the bad news.

The best time to prepare is three years out. Not because you need that long to sell. Because you need that long to fix what buyers actually pay for.

Here is the conversation nobody has early enough.

Buyers do not buy your business. They buy your numbers, your systems, and how easily you can walk away.

If you are the business, you are worth less. If the business runs without you, you are worth more.

I worked with an owner who ran everything himself. Great operator. But every decision went through him. Buyers saw risk, not value.

We spent two years building a manager, cleaning up the books, and documenting how things worked. The sale price went up by a lot.

Three years gives you room to make changes that matter. Six months gives you room to accept whatever you can get.

Start the conversation early.
