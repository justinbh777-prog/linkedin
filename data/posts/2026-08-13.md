# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why waiting until you're burned out is the worst time to sell

---

The best time to sell is when you don't want to.

Sounds backwards. Let me explain.

I have watched hundreds of owners try to sell their franchise. The ones who wait until they're burned out almost always leave money on the table.

Here's why. Burnout shows up in the numbers before it shows up in your bank account.

You stop pushing on sales. You skip the remodel. You let a good manager walk. Buyers see all of it. And they pay less because of it.

Last year I worked with an owner who waited too long. Great business, great location. But he had checked out two years before he called me. Revenue was sliding. Reviews were slipping. He got about 30% less than he would have at his peak.

The lesson is simple. Your business is worth the most when it's running well and you still care about it.

By the time you're desperate to get out, the buyer can feel it. And that costs you.

Sell from strength, not exhaustion.
