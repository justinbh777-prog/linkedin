# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The valuation mistake I see franchise owners make over and over

---

Most franchise owners think they know what their business is worth.

They're usually wrong.

Here's the mistake I see over and over: owners value their business on revenue instead of profit.

They tell me, "We did $2 million in sales last year." Great. But that's not what buyers pay for.

Buyers pay for what's left after the bills. They pay for clean books, real margins, and a business that runs without you.

I had an owner last year who was sure his three units were worth top dollar. Big revenue. But when we dug in, he was the business. He ran operations, handled payroll, covered shifts. Take him out and the profit shrank fast.

That gap between what he thought and what buyers saw cost him real money.

The takeaway is simple. Your business is worth what it earns, not what it sells. And it's worth more when it can run without you in it.

Start fixing that now, even if an exit is years away.
