# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What SBA lenders look for that most sellers never think about

---

Most deals don't fall apart because of the buyer.

They fall apart because of the SBA lender.

Here's what most sellers never think about.

The lender isn't just looking at your revenue. They're looking at how much of your business depends on you.

If you're the reason customers come back, that's a problem. Because when you leave, the lender worries the revenue leaves too.

They want to see systems. A team that runs things. Books that match your tax returns to the dollar.

Had a seller last year with strong numbers. Great location. Loyal customers.

But he did everything himself. No manager. No documented processes.

The lender got nervous. The deal took months longer than it should have, and the buyer nearly walked.

The lesson is simple.

Start building a business that runs without you long before you plan to sell. That's not just good for the sale. It's good for your life right now.

Clean books. A real team. Written processes. That's what makes a lender say yes fast.
