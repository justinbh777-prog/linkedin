# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The difference between franchise transfer and franchise resale

---

"Transfer" and "resale" are not the same thing. Owners mix them up all the time.

Here is the difference.

A transfer is the paperwork. It is the franchisor approving a new owner and signing off on the deal. Every franchise sale needs one.

A resale is the actual sale. It is finding a buyer, pricing the business, negotiating terms, and getting to the closing table.

The transfer is one step inside the bigger resale process. But I have watched owners treat the transfer like it is the whole job.

Last year an owner called me after he had already shaken hands with a buyer. He thought he was almost done. He just needed the transfer approved.

The problem? He never priced his business right. He left real money on the table because he skipped the resale work and jumped straight to the handshake.

The transfer protects the franchisor. The resale protects you.

Do not confuse the two. One keeps the brand happy. The other gets you paid what your business is worth.
