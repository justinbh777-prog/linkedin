# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The conversation every franchise owner should have 3 years before selling

---

Most owners start thinking about selling 6 months too late.

By then, the business is already telling its story. And sometimes it's the wrong one.

Here's what I've learned after 100+ deals.

The owners who get the best price don't start prepping right before they sell. They start three years out.

That's when you can actually change the numbers a buyer cares about.

I worked with an owner once who ran everything himself. Every decision went through him. Great operator. But a buyer looks at that and sees risk, not value.

We spent two years building a team that could run without him. When he finally sold, the business was worth a lot more because it didn't depend on one person.

That's the conversation I wish more owners had early.

Not "what's my business worth today."

But "what do I need to fix now so it's worth more later."

Three years gives you room to clean up the books, build your bench, and grow the right way.

Six months gives you almost nothing.
