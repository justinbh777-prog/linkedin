# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why the best time to think about your exit is right now

---

The best time to sell your franchise is before you're ready to.

I know that sounds backwards. Let me explain.

Most owners come to me when they're burned out, dealing with a health issue, or just done. They want out fast.

The problem? A rushed sale almost always leaves money on the table.

Buyers can smell urgency. They use it against you on price.

The owners who get the best deals are the ones who started thinking about their exit years early. They cleaned up their books. They built systems so the business runs without them. They picked the right time to sell, instead of the market picking it for them.

Last year I worked with an owner who planned ahead for three years. When he finally sold, he walked away with a number that surprised even me.

The takeaway is simple. Planning your exit isn't the same as leaving. It just means you'll be ready when you want to be.

Even if that day is five years out, the work starts now.
