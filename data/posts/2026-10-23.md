# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The real reason most franchise deals fall apart

---

Most franchise deals don't die over price.

They die over trust.

After 100+ closings, I can tell you the number is almost never the real problem. Buyers and sellers usually land close on value. What blows up the deal is what happens after the handshake.

A seller forgets to mention a lease renewal that's three months out. Or the books don't match what they said in the first meeting. Small things. But each one chips away at the buyer's confidence.

I had a deal last year that was all but done. Then the buyer found out the owner hadn't disclosed a staffing issue. Nothing huge. But now he wondered what else wasn't being shared. The deal fell apart in a week.

The lesson: buyers aren't just buying your business. They're buying your honesty.

The cleanest deals I've closed came from owners who put the ugly stuff on the table early. It builds trust instead of breaking it.

So if you're thinking about selling, get your house in order first. Surprises cost you money. Honesty earns it.
