# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The most common thing that kills a franchise deal at the last minute

---

Most franchise deals don't die over price.

They die over paperwork.

After 100+ closings, here's what I know. The number that kills deals at the last minute is almost never the sale number. It's the stuff nobody tracked while the business was running.

Messy books. Missing lease documents. A franchisor transfer approval that nobody started early enough.

I had a deal last year that was fully agreed. Buyer was happy. Seller was ready. Then we asked for clean financials, and the owner had been running personal expenses through the business for years.

The buyer got nervous. The bank got nervous. What should have taken 60 days turned into a fight to keep the deal alive.

We closed it, but at a lower price and with a lot of stress that was avoidable.

Here's the takeaway. The best time to prepare your business for sale is two years before you sell. Clean books. Organized documents. Know your franchisor's transfer rules.

Buyers pay more for a business that's easy to buy.
