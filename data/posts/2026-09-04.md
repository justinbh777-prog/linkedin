# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why your franchisor relationship matters more than you think in a sale

---

Your franchisor can kill your deal. Most owners don't know this until it's too late.

Here's what I've learned after 100+ transactions.

When you sell a franchise, the franchisor has to approve your buyer. That's in your agreement. You don't just sell to the highest bidder. You sell to someone they say yes to.

So your relationship with them matters more than you think.

I worked with an owner last year who had a great buyer ready to go. Strong offer. Clean financials. But the franchisor slowed everything down. Why? The owner had been fighting with them for years over fees and territory.

That bad blood cost him weeks and almost cost him the deal.

Compare that to owners who keep things friendly. Their transfers move fast. The franchisor even helps find buyers sometimes.

Here's the takeaway. Long before you plan to sell, treat your franchisor like a partner in your exit. Because they are.

Pay on time. Communicate. Stay in good standing. It pays off when it counts.
