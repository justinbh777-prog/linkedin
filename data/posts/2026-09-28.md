## VARIANT B — Contrarian Hook

The most successful gym operators aren't just adding more gyms.

They're adding different ones.

A 98-unit Crunch Fitness franchisee just signed a deal to open 25 Yoga Joint studios.

That's a big-box gym operator moving into boutique fitness.

Why? Two words: unit economics.

He liked the model, the design, and the numbers. So he diversified.

Here's what this means if you own 1 to 20 units.

The smart money is spreading risk across concepts that share a customer but not a business model.

One brand slows down, the other picks up. Your income holds steady.

As someone who works with franchise buyers and sellers every day, I'll tell you what buyers pay up for.

Diversification. A mix of brands under one owner is harder to disrupt.

That means a higher multiple when you sell.

Owners locked into one concept are more exposed. Buyers know it, and they price it in.

Three things to watch:

- Whether your brand's growth has stalled or still has room

# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What buyers actually look for that most sellers never prepare

---

Most sellers think buyers care about revenue. They don't.

Buyers care about what happens after you leave.

I've closed over 100 franchise deals in 20+ years. The owners who get the best offers all have one thing in common. Their business runs without them.

Here's what buyers actually look for.

Clean books they can trust. Systems that don't live in your head. A team that stays after the sale. Customers who are loyal to the business, not just to you.

Last year I worked with an owner who ran a great operation. Strong sales. Happy customers. But he was the business. He handled every big client, every key decision.

Buyers saw risk, not value. His offers came in low.

The lesson is simple. If you are the reason the business works, you are also the reason a buyer hesitates.

Start building a business that doesn't need you. Do it a year or two before you sell, not the month you list.

That's the difference between a good exit and a great one.
