# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why waiting until you're burned out is the worst time to sell

---

The best time to sell your franchise is when you still love it.

That sounds backwards. But I've watched it play out for 20 years.

Buyers can smell burnout. When an owner is done, they stop reinvesting. The equipment gets older. The good employees leave. The numbers flatten out.

By the time they call me, the business is worth less than it was two years ago.

Here's what I see over and over. An owner pushes through a rough stretch, tells themselves they'll sell "once things turn around." Then they wait too long, and the only thing that turns around is their energy for running the place.

I had a seller last year who waited one year too many. His revenue dipped, and that dip cost him real money at the closing table. Not because the business was bad. Because he was tired, and it showed.

The owners who get top dollar sell from a position of strength, not exhaustion.

If you're even a little tired, that's the signal to start planning. Not the signal to wait.
