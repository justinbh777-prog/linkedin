# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why some franchise brands are much easier to sell than others

---

Two identical franchises. Same revenue. Same profit.

One sells in 60 days. The other sits for a year.

The difference is almost never the numbers.

After 100+ deals, I can tell you what makes a brand easy to sell.

Buyers want a brand they have heard of. National name recognition moves deals fast. A regional brand nobody knows makes buyers nervous, even when the profit is strong.

They also want franchisor support. When the corporate team helps with training and transfers, buyers feel safe. When the franchisor is slow or difficult, deals die.

And they want a simple business. Fewer employees. Less specialized labor. Easy to learn. That sells.

I once had two owners with nearly the same numbers. One was in a household-name food brand. The other ran a niche service brand most people had never heard of.

The food brand had three offers in a week. The other took ten months.

Here is the takeaway. The brand on your sign affects your exit as much as your bottom line.

Know this before you sell.
