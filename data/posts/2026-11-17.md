# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why building a business that runs without you is the best exit strategy

---

The best time to sell your franchise is when you don't have to be there.

I've closed over 100 deals in 20 years. The pattern is always the same.

The owners who get the highest multiples are the ones who built a business that runs without them.

Buyers aren't just buying revenue. They're buying freedom from you.

If the business falls apart the day you leave, that's a risk. And buyers pay less for risk.

I once had two owners with almost identical numbers. Same revenue. Same unit count. Same market.

One worked 60 hours a week and ran everything himself. The other had a strong manager and systems in place.

The second owner sold for nearly 40% more. Same business on paper. Very different buyer confidence.

Here's the takeaway. Start building your exit years before you plan to leave.

Hire the manager. Write the processes. Step back on purpose.

You'll run a better business while you own it, and sell it for more when you're ready.
