# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why franchise owners underestimate how long a sale takes

---

"I want to sell by summer."

I hear this a lot. Usually in January.

Here is the hard truth. A franchise sale takes 6 to 12 months. Sometimes longer.

Most owners think it moves like selling a house. List it, get an offer, close in 30 days.

It does not work that way.

There is the valuation. The financials you need to clean up. The franchisor approval. The buyer financing. The lease transfer. Each step takes time, and they do not all happen at once.

Last year I worked with an owner who wanted out fast. His books were a mess. We spent three months just getting his numbers buyer-ready before we could even go to market.

That delay cost him nothing in the end. But it could have.

Here is the takeaway. The best time to prepare is before you want to sell. Not when you are already tired and ready to walk.

If your exit is a year or two out, start now. Clean books and clear records make everything faster later.
