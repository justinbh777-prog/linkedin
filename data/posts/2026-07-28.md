# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The most common thing that kills a franchise deal at the last minute

---

Most deals don't die over price.

They die over the lease.

I've watched fully agreed deals fall apart in the final two weeks because nobody checked the lease early enough.

Here's what happens. The buyer is ready. The price is set. Everyone's excited.

Then the landlord gets involved.

Maybe they won't approve the transfer. Maybe they want a personal guarantee the buyer won't sign. Maybe the lease only has 18 months left and the franchisor requires five years.

Now the deal stalls. The buyer gets cold feet. The momentum you spent months building is gone.

I had a seller last year with a great business and a strong offer. The lease had a hidden clause requiring landlord consent with no time limit. It took nine weeks to sort out. The buyer almost walked twice.

The lesson is simple. Read your lease before you list. Know your renewal terms. Know what the landlord can and can't do.

The best time to fix a lease problem is before a buyer ever sees your business.
