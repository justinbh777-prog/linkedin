# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The most common thing that kills a franchise deal at the last minute

---

Most franchise deals don't die over price.

They die over the lease.

After 20 years and 100+ closings, I can tell you the number one deal killer at the finish line is the landlord.

Here's what happens. The buyer and seller agree on everything. Terms, price, timeline. Everyone is ready to sign.

Then we need the landlord to approve the lease transfer. And suddenly nothing moves.

The landlord wants a personal guarantee. Or a rent increase. Or they take three weeks to return a call. Meanwhile the buyer gets nervous and starts to walk.

I had a deal last year where the seller was two weeks from closing. Clean business. Great buyer. The landlord sat on the paperwork for a month, and the buyer's financing timed out. The deal fell apart over something that had nothing to do with the business itself.

The lesson? Pull your lease early. Know how many years you have left. Talk to your landlord before you talk to a buyer.

Fix this before it costs you the sale.
