# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What buyers pay a premium for that has nothing to do with revenue

---

Two franchises. Same revenue. One sold for 40% more.

The difference had nothing to do with the numbers.

Here is what I have learned after 100+ deals. Buyers pay a premium for businesses that can run without the owner.

That is it. That is the secret.

When I evaluate a franchise, one of the first things I look for is how much the business depends on you. Who handles hiring? Who closes the big accounts? Who do customers call when something goes wrong?

If the answer is always "the owner," the price goes down.

I once had two clients in the same brand. Similar locations. Similar sales. One had a general manager running day to day operations. The other was the glue holding everything together.

The first one sold fast and for more. The second one sat on the market because buyers saw a job, not an asset.

The takeaway is simple. Start stepping back now. Build a team that does not need you. That work pays off the day you sell.
