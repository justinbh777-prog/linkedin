# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The most common thing that kills a franchise deal at the last minute

---

Most deals don't die over price.

They die over the lease.

After 20+ years and 100+ closings, I can tell you the lease is the silent deal killer. Everyone focuses on the numbers. Then the landlord shows up at the finish line.

The buyer needs the lease transferred or a new one signed. The landlord sees a chance to squeeze more rent. Or they drag their feet for weeks. Or they want a huge personal guarantee.

Suddenly a clean deal stalls out.

I had a seller last year with a great business and a ready buyer. We were days from closing. The landlord went quiet for three weeks, then asked for terms that scared the buyer off. We saved it, but it took real work.

Here is the takeaway.

Call your landlord early. Understand your lease terms before you list. Know what transfer rights you actually have.

The best time to fix a lease problem is before a buyer is even in the picture.

Don't let the one thing you forgot about undo months of work.
