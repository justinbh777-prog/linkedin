# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What buyers actually look for that most sellers never prepare

---

Most sellers think buyers care about revenue.

They don't. Not first, anyway.

After 100+ transactions, here's what I've learned. Buyers care about how much the business depends on you.

If you are the reason it runs, you are the risk they are buying. And risk lowers your price.

I worked with an owner last year who ran three units. Strong sales. Good margins. But he made every hire, handled every problem, and knew every customer by name.

Buyers loved the numbers. Then they saw the owner was the business. Offers came in low.

We spent six months building a manager layer and clean systems. He stepped back. Sales held.

The business sold for a lot more than it would have the year before. Same units. Same brand. Different risk.

Here is the takeaway. Buyers pay for a business that runs without you.

The best time to build that is before you ever plan to sell. Not the month you list.
