# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What I wish every franchise owner knew about SBA financing

---

Most franchise deals don't fall apart over price. They fall apart over SBA financing.

Here's what I wish every owner understood before they sell.

Buyers are usually borrowing most of the money. That means the bank has to approve your business before the buyer ever can.

And banks look at your books differently than you do.

They want clean financials. They want to see real cash flow. They want tax returns that match what you've been telling people.

If your paperwork is messy, the loan gets smaller or the deal dies.

I had a seller with a great business and a serious buyer. The problem was three years of sloppy bookkeeping. The bank cut the loan amount, and the buyer walked.

That deal was lost before we ever talked price.

The takeaway is simple. Your exit starts years before you sell. Clean books today protect your payout tomorrow.

If you run your business like you might sell it, you'll always have options.
