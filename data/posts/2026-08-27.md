# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why franchise owners underestimate how long a sale takes

---

"I want to sell by summer."

I hear this every year. Usually in January.

Here is the hard truth. A franchise sale takes 6 to 12 months. Sometimes longer.

Most owners think it works like selling a house. List it, get an offer, close in 30 days. It does not work that way.

Buyers need financing. Lenders need documents. Your franchisor needs to approve the new owner. Each step takes weeks, not days.

Last year I worked with an owner who wanted a fast exit. Great business. Clean books. Still took 9 months from our first call to the closing table.

Nothing went wrong. That is just how long it takes when you do it right.

Here is what I tell every owner. The best time to start is before you are ready to sell.

Get your numbers clean. Understand your value. Know your timeline. Then you are not rushing when the moment comes.

Selling in a hurry almost always costs you money.
