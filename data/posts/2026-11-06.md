# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What happens when a franchise owner's lease runs out during a sale

---

I've watched deals fall apart over something most owners never think about.

The lease.

Here's what happens. An owner decides to sell. They've got a great business, solid numbers, a buyer ready to go.

Then we check the lease. It has 14 months left.

No buyer wants to pay full price for a business that might lose its location in a year. And no lender wants to fund it either.

I saw this recently with a food franchise doing strong revenue. The owner thought they were months from closing. The short lease cut the offers way down and scared off two buyers completely.

The fix is simple but it takes time. You talk to the landlord before you list. You get an extension or a renewal option in writing.

A buyer wants to see five or ten years of runway. Not a lease running out the door.

So if selling is even on your radar, look at your lease now.

The value you built deserves a location that comes with it.
