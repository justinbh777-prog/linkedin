# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What 100+ franchise transactions taught me about what buyers really want

---

Buyers don't buy your business. They buy your absence.

That surprised me when I started closing deals 20 years ago. I thought buyers wanted strong revenue above everything.

They don't.

What they really want is a business that runs without you.

Over 100+ transactions, I've watched the same pattern play out. Two franchises with nearly identical numbers. One sells fast at a premium. The other sits.

The difference is almost never the money.

I once worked with an owner doing great revenue, but he was involved in everything. Every hire. Every schedule. Every customer complaint. Buyers walked because they weren't buying a business, they were buying his job.

Another owner made less money but had a manager running daily operations. He sold in weeks.

Here's the takeaway.

If you want top dollar someday, start removing yourself now. Build the systems. Train the manager. Document how things work.

The less the business needs you, the more it's worth.

The best time to do this is two or three years before you sell, not two or three months.
