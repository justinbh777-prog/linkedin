# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why the best time to think about your exit is right now

---

Most owners wait too long to think about selling.

By the time they call me, they've already decided to leave. Now they're in a hurry. And a rushed sale almost never gets top dollar.

Here's what I've learned after 100+ deals.

The best exits are planned two or three years out. Not two or three months.

When you have time, you can clean up your books. You can fix the things buyers pick at. You can grow the numbers that actually move your valuation.

I worked with an owner last year who wanted out fast. Solid business. But messy records and one location carrying the whole group. We could have sold it. It just would have left real money on the table.

So we waited. Ten months later, cleaner financials and a stronger story. The offer came in much higher.

The lesson is simple.

Thinking about your exit early is not the same as selling. It just means you're ready when the right moment comes.

You keep the leverage. Not the buyer.
