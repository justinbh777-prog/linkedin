# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What 100+ franchise transactions taught me about what buyers really want

---

Buyers don't buy your business. They buy the version of it that runs without you.

After 100+ franchise deals, that's the biggest lesson I keep learning.

Owners always want to talk about revenue. And revenue matters. But it's not the first thing a serious buyer asks about.

They ask who runs the day-to-day. They ask what happens if you go on vacation for a month. They ask how much of the business lives inside your head.

If the answer is "everything depends on me," the offers come in lower. Every time.

I once had a seller with strong numbers who couldn't step away for a single week. Great operator. Hard sell. We spent six months building a team and clean systems before we listed. The final price was much higher because of it.

Here's the takeaway. The most valuable thing you can do before selling is make yourself less necessary.

Start now. Even if you're years away from an exit.
