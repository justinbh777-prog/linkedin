# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The three numbers every franchise owner should know before they sell

---

Most franchise owners can tell me their revenue in two seconds.

Then I ask three other questions and the room goes quiet.

Here are the three numbers that actually decide what your business sells for.

1. Seller's Discretionary Earnings.

This is your real profit plus the perks you run through the business. Buyers care about this more than revenue. A high-revenue store with thin margins is worth less than you think.

2. Your owner dependency score.

If the business needs you there every day, that is a problem. The more it runs without you, the more a buyer will pay. I have seen two stores with identical profit sell for very different prices because of this one factor.

3. Your remaining franchise term.

A buyer wants runway. If you have two years left on your agreement, that scares people. Ten years is a different conversation.

I once had an owner shocked that his "smaller" competitor sold for more. The competitor knew all three numbers. He only knew one.

Know these before you list. Not after.
