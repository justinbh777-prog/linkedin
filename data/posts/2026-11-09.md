# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why franchise owners underestimate how long a sale takes

---

"Can we close by the end of the quarter?"

I hear this almost every time an owner first calls me.

The honest answer? A franchise sale usually takes 6 to 12 months. Sometimes longer.

Most owners think it moves like selling a house. List it, get an offer, close. It does not work that way.

There is due diligence. There is franchisor approval, which nobody talks about but can add weeks on its own. There is financing. There is the buyer getting cold feet and warming back up.

Last year I worked with an owner who wanted out fast. Great business. Clean books. Even then it took nine months from our first call to the wire transfer.

He was not slow. The process is just long.

Here is the takeaway. If you want to sell, start the conversation early. The owners who get the best price are the ones who planned 12 to 18 months ahead. The ones who rush usually leave money on the table.

Selling is a process, not an event.
