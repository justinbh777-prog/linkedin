# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** A mistake franchise owners make when thinking about selling

---

Most owners wait too long to think about selling.

They decide they want out, then try to sell six months later. That timing almost never works in their favor.

Here is what I see.

The best time to sell is when your business is growing and you still have energy for it. Strong numbers. Clean books. A story buyers get excited about.

But most people wait until they are tired. Until revenue dips. Until they are ready to be done yesterday.

By then the business shows it. Buyers can smell burnout, and they pay less for it.

I worked with an owner last year who ran three solid units. Good locations. Good staff. But he had checked out two years earlier, and the numbers showed the slide.

He left real money on the table. Not because the business was bad, but because he waited until he had nothing left to give.

The lesson is simple. Start thinking about your exit while things are still good, not when you are done.

The best deals I close start years before the sale.
