# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why building a business that runs without you is the best exit strategy

---

The buyer walked away. Not because of the numbers. Because the business couldn't run without the owner.

I've seen this kill more deals than bad financials ever could.

Here's what most owners miss. When you're the one making every decision, the one every employee calls, the one who knows all the vendors by name, you don't own a business. You own a job.

And buyers don't want to buy your job.

I worked with an owner who ran three locations. Great revenue. Loyal customers. But he opened the store most mornings and signed off on everything.

When buyers asked what happens if he takes a two-week vacation, he laughed. He hadn't taken one in six years.

That answer cost him on price.

The owners who sell for the most have one thing in common. They've made themselves replaceable.

They have managers who run the day-to-day. Systems written down. Teams that don't need them for every small thing.

Start building that now. Not the year you want to sell.

The business that runs without you is the one worth the most when you're ready to leave.
