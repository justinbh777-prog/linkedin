# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What 100+ franchise transactions taught me about what buyers really want

---

Most buyers aren't buying your business. They're buying their freedom from a job.

That changes everything about what they look for.

After 100+ franchise deals, I can tell you the owners who sell fast have one thing in common. The business runs without them.

Buyers get nervous when they see an owner who does everything. Opens the store. Handles the schedule. Knows every customer by name.

That sounds like a strength. To a buyer, it looks like a trap they can't escape.

I had a seller last year with great revenue and strong margins. But he was the business. Every system lived in his head.

We spent six months building a management layer and writing down his processes. Same numbers. Very different story.

He sold for a higher price and had multiple offers.

Here's the takeaway. If you want to sell well someday, start removing yourself now. Document how things work. Build a team that doesn't need you.

The best time to do this is years before you sell.
