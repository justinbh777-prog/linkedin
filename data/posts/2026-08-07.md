# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What buyers actually look for that most sellers never prepare

---

Most sellers think buyers care about revenue.

They don't. Not first, anyway.

After 100+ deals, I can tell you what buyers actually look at. They want to know if the business runs without you.

If you are the reason it works, that scares them. It means they are not buying a business. They are buying a job.

I had a seller last year with strong numbers. Great location. But he touched everything. Scheduling, hiring, key accounts, all him.

Buyers walked. The ones who stayed offered way less than he expected.

Compare that to an owner with lower revenue but a solid manager and clean systems. That business sold fast, and for a premium.

Here is the takeaway.

Buyers pay for what they can step into. A trained team. Documented processes. Numbers they can trust without a shoebox of receipts.

Start building that now, even if a sale is years away. The work you do today shows up in your price later.

The best time to prepare was five years ago. The second best time is today.

If you are thinking about an exit - even years from now - let's talk.
