# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The difference between franchise transfer and franchise resale

---

Most franchise owners think "transfer" and "resale" mean the same thing.

They don't. And the difference can cost you.

A transfer is just the paperwork. Moving the franchise agreement from your name to a new owner. The franchisor approves it. That's it.

A resale is the actual deal. Finding the right buyer. Setting the right price. Structuring terms that protect you. Then the transfer happens at the end.

Here's what I see too often.

An owner finds a buyer on their own. They shake hands on a number. Then they call the franchisor to "transfer" it.

They left money on the table because they skipped the resale part. No competition for the business. No real valuation. No one negotiating on their side.

I worked with an owner who almost sold for a handshake number. We took it to market instead. Final price came in well above what he first agreed to.

The lesson is simple. The transfer is the last step, not the deal itself.

If you skip the resale work, you're leaving your exit up to luck.
