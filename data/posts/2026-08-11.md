# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** A mistake franchise owners make when thinking about selling

---

Most owners wait too long to sell.

Not because they don't want to. Because they think they need to wait for the "perfect" year.

Here is what I see all the time.

An owner has a great year. Revenue is up. Profit looks strong. So they decide to wait one more year to sell even higher.

Then the next year is flat. Or a lease issue pops up. Or a bigger competitor moves in down the street.

Now they are selling into a weaker story, not a stronger one.

I worked with an owner who turned down a solid offer because he wanted "one more record year." Two years later he sold for less. The market shifted and so did his numbers.

The truth is simple. Buyers pay for momentum and confidence, not just one big year.

The best time to sell is when your business is climbing and your story is clean. Not when you are tired or forced to.

You do not need to sell now. But you should know your options before the decision gets made for you.
