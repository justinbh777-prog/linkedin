# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The difference between owners who get top dollar and those who don't

---

Two owners. Same brand. Same number of units.

One sold for 40% more than the other.

The difference had nothing to do with luck.

The seller who got top dollar started preparing two years before he listed. Clean books. Documented systems. A manager who could run the place without him.

The other owner called me on a Friday and wanted to sell by the end of the month. His numbers were a mess. He was the business. Take him out and there was nothing left to buy.

Buyers pay for confidence. When they can see exactly what they are getting, they pay more. When they have to guess, they lower the price to protect themselves.

Here is what I tell every owner I work with.

Your exit starts long before the sale. The value you get is built in the years before you ever pick up the phone.

If you wait until you are tired and ready to walk, you have already left money on the table.

The best time to prepare is when you are not ready to sell.
