# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What 20 years of franchise resale transactions has taught me

---

Most franchise owners wait too long to sell.

After 20 years and 100+ transactions, that is the pattern I see most.

Here is the truth. The best time to sell is when your business is growing, not when you are tired of running it.

Buyers pay for momentum. They pay for clean books, strong staff, and a location that is trending up.

But most owners wait until they are burned out. By then the numbers are slipping, and the story is harder to tell.

I once worked with an owner who ran three units. He was exhausted and wanted out fast. If he had called me two years earlier, when sales were climbing, I believe he would have walked away with a lot more.

He still did fine. But timing cost him.

So here is what I tell owners. Do not think about selling only when you want out. Think about it while things are good. That is when you have leverage.

Your exit is a strategy, not a reaction.
