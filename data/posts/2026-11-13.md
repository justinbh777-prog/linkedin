# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why owner-dependent businesses always sell for less

---

Here is a hard truth about selling your franchise.

If the business can't run without you, it's worth less. Sometimes a lot less.

Buyers aren't just buying your revenue. They're buying what happens after you leave.

And when they look at your business and see that you are the one holding everything together, they get nervous. Nervous buyers pay less.

I had a seller last year with great numbers. Strong location. Loyal customers. But he did everything himself. Scheduling, hiring, vendor calls, even covering shifts.

The buyers loved the business. They just didn't love that it was really a job with his name on it.

The offers came in lower than they should have. Not because the business was bad, but because the risk sat entirely on him.

Here is the takeaway.

Start building a business that runs without you now. Train a manager. Write down your systems. Step back on purpose.

The owners who get the best offers are the ones who made themselves optional long before they sold.
