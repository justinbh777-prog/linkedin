# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What happens when a franchise owner's lease runs out during a sale

---

Your lease could kill your deal. Most owners don't see it coming.

Here's what I mean.

When you sell a franchise, the buyer isn't just buying your business. They're buying the right to keep operating in that location.

If your lease has 18 months left and no options to renew, that's a problem.

Buyers get nervous. Lenders get nervous. Deals stall or the price drops.

I had a seller last year with a great location and strong numbers. But his lease was almost up. The landlord was slow to respond, and the buyer started to walk.

We saved it, but only because we caught it early and pushed the landlord hard for an extension.

Here's the takeaway.

Look at your lease before you list. Not during the sale. Before.

If you have less than three years left, start talking to your landlord now about renewal or extension options.

A clean, transferable lease makes your business worth more. A messy one scares buyers off.
