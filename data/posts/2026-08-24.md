# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The real reason most franchise deals fall apart

---

Most franchise deals don't die over price.

They die over trust.

After 100+ closed transactions, I can tell you the number one deal killer isn't the valuation. It's what shows up after the handshake.

The seller says revenue is clean. Then the buyer's team starts digging and finds messy books, expired leases, or a key manager who's about to walk.

Every surprise chips away at trust. And once a buyer stops trusting the seller, they start questioning everything. The price. The projections. The whole deal.

I had a seller last year with a strong, profitable business. Great location. Good numbers. But he waited until due diligence to mention a lawsuit from an old employee.

It wasn't a big lawsuit. But hiding it made the buyer wonder what else was hidden. The deal fell apart in a week.

Here's the takeaway. Buyers don't need a perfect business. They need an honest one.

Put your problems on the table early. Every single one. Deals survive bad news. They rarely survive surprises.
