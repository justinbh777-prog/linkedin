# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The real reason most franchise deals fall apart

---

Most franchise deals don't fall apart over price.

They fall apart over trust.

After 100+ transactions, I've watched deals with perfect numbers collapse at the finish line. And deals with average numbers close smoothly.

The difference is almost always the same thing.

The seller wasn't ready to be honest.

Not lying, exactly. Just holding things back. A lease issue they hoped no one would notice. Numbers that looked better on paper than in the bank account. A key employee who was already halfway out the door.

Buyers can feel when something is off. And the moment they stop trusting you, they start looking for reasons to walk.

I had a deal last year where the seller waited until week six to mention a pending equipment problem. Small issue. Cheap fix. But the buyer walked anyway.

Not because of the equipment. Because they wondered what else was being hidden.

Here's the takeaway.

Get everything on the table early. The good, the bad, the messy. Clean information sells. Surprises kill deals.

The best sellers I work with treat honesty as their strongest negotiating tool.

If you are thinking about an exit - even years from now - let's talk.
