# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What I tell every franchise owner who calls me in a panic to sell fast

---

"Sell fast" is usually the worst thing a franchise owner can do.

The panic calls all sound the same.

Something happened. A health scare. A partner dispute. Burnout that finally hit a wall. And now they want out yesterday.

Here is what I tell all of them.

Buyers can smell desperation. The second they know you need to sell fast, your price drops. Sometimes by a lot.

A rushed sale also means messy books, no clean story, and no time to fix the small stuff that scares buyers away.

I had an owner call me ready to dump his three units at almost any number. We slowed down for 90 days. Cleaned up the financials. Fixed one underperforming location. He sold for close to 40 percent more than his panic price.

The lesson is simple. The best time to prepare for a sale is before you need one.

If you wait until you are desperate, the market knows. And it costs you.

So start early. Even if you are years away.
