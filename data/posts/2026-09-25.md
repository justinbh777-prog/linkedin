# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** A mistake franchise owners make when thinking about selling

---

Most owners wait too long to sell.

Not because they're greedy. Because they're waiting for the "perfect" year.

Here's what I see over and over.

An owner has a great year. Revenue is up. Profit looks strong. So they decide to wait one more year to sell at the top.

Then something shifts. A new competitor opens. Labor costs jump. Their best manager quits. Suddenly that peak year is in the rearview mirror, and the business is worth less than it was 12 months ago.

I worked with an owner last year who turned down a strong offer because he wanted "one more record year." That year never came. When he finally sold, he left real money on the table.

The truth is simple. The best time to sell is usually before you feel ready.

Buyers pay for momentum. They pay for a business that still has room to grow. If you wait until every number peaks, you've often sold the upside they were willing to pay for.

Don't time the top. Sell into strength.
