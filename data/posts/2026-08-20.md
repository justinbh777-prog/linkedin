# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** Why some franchise brands are much easier to sell than others

---

Two franchises. Same revenue. One sold in 30 days. The other sat for a year.

The difference had nothing to do with the owners.

It was the brand.

After 100+ deals, I can usually tell how hard a sale will be before I even see the numbers.

Buyers ask about the brand first. Is it growing? Is the franchisor easy to work with? Are new units opening or quietly closing?

If the answers are good, buyers line up. If they are not, the deal drags no matter how strong your location is.

I had two clients last year in the same city. Similar profit. One had a hot brand with a waitlist of buyers. The other had a brand that stopped marketing and lost momentum.

The first owner had three offers in a month. The second one is still waiting.

Here is the takeaway. Your exit value is tied to a brand you do not fully control. So pay attention to where your franchisor is headed, not just where your own store stands.
