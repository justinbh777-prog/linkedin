# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The three numbers every franchise owner should know before they sell

---

Most franchise owners have no idea what their business is actually worth.

They know their sales. Maybe their profit. That's usually where it stops.

After 100+ transactions, I've learned there are three numbers that decide everything.

First: your SDE. That's seller's discretionary earnings. It's your real profit plus your salary, your perks, and the one-time stuff buyers add back. This is what your business gets valued on. Not revenue.

Second: your multiple. Franchise resales usually sell for a range based on brand strength, unit count, and how well the business runs without you. Two owners with the same profit can sell for very different prices.

Third: your add-backs. The personal expenses running through the business that a buyer won't have. Done right, these can raise your value by six figures. Done wrong, they raise red flags.

I once had an owner convinced his store was worth 4x what a buyer would pay. He was looking at revenue. Once we ran the real numbers, the conversation changed completely.

Know these three before you ever list.
