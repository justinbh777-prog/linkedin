# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The most common thing that kills a franchise deal at the last minute

---

Most franchise deals don't die over price.

They die over the lease.

After 100+ transactions, I've watched more deals fall apart in the final week because of a landlord than any other single reason.

Here's what happens. The buyer is ready. The financing is approved. Everyone is excited.

Then the lease assignment hits the landlord's desk. And suddenly they want a personal guarantee, a rent bump, or two months to "review."

The clock keeps ticking. The buyer gets nervous. The deal loses momentum.

I had a seller last year with a clean business and a strong offer. The landlord sat on the assignment for six weeks. By the time they responded, the buyer had walked.

The lesson is simple. Your landlord is a silent partner in your exit, and most owners don't find that out until it's too late.

If you're thinking about selling in the next year or two, pull out your lease now. Know your assignment terms. Talk to your landlord before a buyer ever shows up.
