# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What I wish every franchise owner knew about SBA financing

---

Most franchise deals don't die over price.

They die over SBA financing.

Here's what I wish every owner knew before they tried to sell.

Your buyer probably needs an SBA loan. And the bank isn't just looking at your buyer. They're looking at your business too.

Clean books matter more than you think. If your tax returns don't match your P&L, the loan slows down or dies.

The bank also cares about your lease. A short lease with no renewal options can kill a deal, because the loan term has to fit inside the lease term.

I had a seller last year with a great business and a strong buyer. The deal almost fell apart because his lease had 18 months left. We had to go back to the landlord before the bank would move.

The takeaway is simple.

Start cleaning up your books and your lease years before you sell. Not months.

The buyers will come. The financing is what makes or breaks the close.
