# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What happens when a franchise owner's lease runs out during a sale

---

Your lease might be worth more than your business.

Let me explain.

I've watched deals fall apart in the final weeks because of one thing nobody checked early: the lease.

A buyer isn't just buying your business. They're buying your location. And if your lease is close to running out, that's a problem.

Here's what happens. Buyer gets excited. Numbers look great. Then they ask the landlord about a new lease, and suddenly the rent jumps 30 percent. Or the landlord won't renew at all.

Now the deal is stuck. The buyer gets nervous. Your sale price drops.

I had a seller with a strong store. Good revenue. Loyal customers. But the lease had 14 months left and no renewal option. We lost two buyers before we fixed it.

The lesson is simple. Deal with your lease before you list, not during the sale.

Talk to your landlord early. Lock in an extension or a renewal option. A clean lease makes your business easier to sell and worth more.

Most owners never think about this until it's too late.
