# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What buyers pay a premium for that has nothing to do with revenue

---

The highest offer I ever got a client wasn't about revenue.

It was about how little the owner was needed.

Buyers don't just buy your sales. They buy your freedom. And the less a business depends on you, the more they'll pay.

I've watched two nearly identical shops sell for very different numbers. Same revenue. Same location type. Same margins.

One owner worked 60 hours a week and knew every customer by name. The other had a manager running the whole thing and checked in twice a week.

Guess which one sold for more.

The hands-off business went for a much higher multiple. Because the buyer saw a machine, not a job.

If you're the glue holding everything together, that's a red flag to a buyer. It means the value walks out the door when you do.

So build the systems now. Train the manager. Make yourself replaceable.

You'll run a calmer business today and a far more valuable one tomorrow.
