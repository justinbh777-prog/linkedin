# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** What SBA lenders look for that most sellers never think about

---

Most deals don't die because of the buyer.

They die because of the lender.

Here's what most sellers never think about: the SBA lender is the real gatekeeper. And they look at things you'd never expect.

They don't just care about your revenue. They care about how clean your books are.

If your tax returns don't match your P&L, that's a problem. If you run personal expenses through the business, that's a problem too.

Lenders also look at customer concentration. If one client is 40% of your sales, they get nervous. What happens if that client leaves after the sale?

I had a seller last year with a great business. Strong profit. Loyal staff. But his financials were a mess. Add-backs everywhere. No documentation.

The buyer wanted it. The bank said no. The deal took four extra months just to clean things up.

Here's the takeaway. Start thinking like a lender two years before you sell.

Clean books. Clear records. Fewer surprises.

That's what gets deals funded.
