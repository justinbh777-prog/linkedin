# POST 2 — Personal Post

**Topic:** The real reason most franchise deals fall apart

---

Most franchise deals don't fall apart over price.

They fall apart over trust.

After 100+ transactions, I can tell you the number one deal killer isn't the valuation. It's what shows up after the handshake.

The seller forgot to mention the lease is up in eight months.

The books don't match the tax returns.

Three employees quit the week due diligence started.

None of these are huge problems on their own. But a buyer who feels surprised starts to wonder what else is hiding. And once trust cracks, the whole deal gets shaky.

I had a seller last year with a strong business and a serious buyer. The deal died because he waited to disclose a supplier dispute. The buyer found out on his own. Game over.

Here's the takeaway: buyers don't expect your business to be perfect. They expect you to be honest.

Lay the problems on the table early. You'll keep more deals alive and often get a better price for being upfront.

The clean deal isn't the one with no issues. It's the one with no surprises.
